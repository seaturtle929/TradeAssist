# Trade Assist

Private English sales copilot for manufacturer representatives. Listening transcribes the conversation; **Suggest next move** produces coaching when you ask. You do the talking.

## Start on this Mac

Double-click **Start Trade Assist.command**. It runs in the background and opens **http://127.0.0.1:5173/**. Terminal may be closed. Use `node scripts/stop-local.mjs` to stop it. Use Node 22.13+ or the bundled runtime.

1. Add a manufacturer and product categories in **Company knowledge**.
2. Paste an anyone-with-the-link Google Drive file or folder link. Read the preview, then choose **Save to company memory**. This saves the source library; imported text does not become active copilot knowledge until you review and apply it in Company Brain. Linked sources retain their original URL and read date. **Refresh** reads the original again and shows a preview before replacing saved text. Manual text and PDF uploads remain available.
3. Managers open **Company Brain** to edit **What we know**, **How we sell** and **Our boundaries**, one section at a time. Add or approve company facts, configure each stage’s objective/playbook/information to bring back, and define company-wide or stage-specific authority. Edits save as a draft. Choose **Review changes**, resolve conflicts and **Apply configuration** before starting a new call. In **Connection & audio**, choose your tone and phrases to avoid. **Edit wording & teach my voice** saves a style example; examples never establish company facts or commercial authority. Enable **Bilingual mode** for Chinese coaching with an English script.
4. Open **Live Assistant** and select a client or choose **Add client**. Add client opens Call setup directly: enter a company name, optionally its main-line phone, then choose a manager-configured sales stage. Its objective appears immediately. Add named contacts later; email-only contacts are supported. In **Background for this call**, keep reusable context, add new developments, paste emails or upload `.eml`, `.txt`, `.md`, `.html` or `.pdf` files. **Prepare brief / Update brief** reads accessible public HTTPS pages and supported files. Review the editable Situation, Potential fit and What to clarify, with dated evidence on demand. Failed sources stay visible. Confirm preparation with one checkbox; optional background and briefs may be left empty.
5. Choose **Computer call audio · Mac / browser** and use headphones. Call playback is labeled **Buyer**; the separately selected headset microphone is labeled **You**. With your AT&T iPhone, enable **Calls on Other Devices**, then **Calls from iPhone** in FaceTime on the Mac. Dial the buyer's ordinary phone number through the iPhone cellular line using FaceTime. This keeps your carrier number. In an up-to-date Chrome, test computer audio and choose **Entire Screen → Share system audio**. The app rejects a share without audio. The local test shows separate Buyer and You meters. After the test, choose **Start listening** and share audio again. Listening updates the transcript without generating suggestions. Click **Suggest next move** or press **Alt+Enter** (⌥ Enter on Mac) to request guidance. The shortcut is inactive while typing in editable fields. You may request an opener before audio or buyer speech, or ask for help at any later point. Speakerphone and headset microphone modes remain available.
6. Choose **End conversation & review**. Edit the recap/outcome, select the memory changes and newly captured contacts to keep, and optionally update the client category. **Save call** preserves the dated session and confirmed client updates together. A promise remains agreed until completion is supported. **New call** also opens this review if a conversation is unfinished. **Call History** lists clients first, then their individual dated sessions with contact, stage, preparation, transcript and manager review. Generate the Manager Decision Packet explicitly when needed; it never sends email.

Before a call, **Opening preference** optionally lets you choose **Write my own** to draft exact wording and **Save as company default** to reuse it for that manufacturer. Otherwise the copilot chooses an opener only when requested. The saved opening is available to **Suggest next move**, which adapts the next action to the selected stage and actual conversation. Unsaved choices reset on a new call; saved defaults belong to the company and survive reload/export. Preparing or saving wording does not verify product claims or record anything as spoken.

Client records have stable IDs scoped to the selling company. Live Assistant and Call History share the same All, Developing, Existing customers and Inactive filters. New clients start unassessed. Category describes the relationship; stage describes this conversation, so an existing customer may be in Technical evaluation. Inactive clients can retain a reason and a condition for reopening. A corrected display name does not merge clients or rewrite original call labels.

Reusable background and source files remain with the client. **New developments for this call** adds current information without replacing earlier transcripts. A subsequent email can establish that previously promised materials were sent, while a new promise does not. Sender, recipient, date and available thread metadata stay with parsed email sources. Website observations cannot establish buyer interest or our company’s capabilities, and client context cannot override manager boundaries. Background changes invalidate the reviewed context and visibly mark an older brief as needing an update; manually reviewing current background is still allowed without regenerating a brief.

Each new call has its own session, preparation snapshot and configuration version. Resuming an unfinished draft restores that same session; repeated save updates that record rather than duplicating it. Starting listening collapses preparation into an accessible summary, retaining client/contact, stage and objective. **Suggest next move** remains explicit and stable while either participant speaks. On the next call, choose the contact and stage, add developments, and review again. Migration preserves calls, contacts and memory; ambiguous legacy associations are flagged for review rather than silently merged.

Use **Remove client** beside a client in Live Assistant or Call History, then confirm. Removal hides the client from the active list and keeps its contacts, context and saved calls. Expand **Removed clients** to view its history or **Restore client**. Save an unfinished call before removing its client; live calls must be stopped first. Removal and restoration survive reload and backup imports.

## Knowledge and imports

Before a first-contact cold call, request an opener with **Suggest next move** after reviewing preparation. Optional custom opening wording remains available under **Opening wording preference**. Preparation research is requested separately through **Prepare brief**; it does not generate live coaching. Opening observations use reviewed client evidence, and a manufacturer capability requires active, approved Company Brain knowledge. Preparing or copying wording never creates transcript speech or buyer agreement.

**Call progress → Buyer understanding** shows current use, stated difficulties, practical effects, priorities, approval criteria, timing and supplier context with exact evidence. Prepared context and earlier contacts are labeled separately. Interest remains unstated until the buyer expresses it. The copilot follows the buyer's answer with at most one useful question, avoids answered/declined topics, gives room to think and does not force a replacement/backup/quote choice. Having a supplier alone does not establish a problem. Agreed materials/review steps remain remembered, while a new buyer question, objection or correction can interrupt them. Each new call has separate needs and commitments.

Buyer transcript lines automatically show a Simplified Mandarin translation beneath the original. Use **中文翻译** in the Conversation header or Connection & audio to show or hide them. Completed **Call audio** lines also translate in mixed microphone modes without changing their speaker label; separately labeled **You** lines remain untranslated. New live lines have a dedicated single-line queue, while restored draft text catches up separately in small batches. Translation runs separately from response coaching and counts toward existing spending limits. Finished translations are kept with the call and backup. The original transcript remains the source for coaching, buyer evidence and commitments; translations do not become words spoken on the call.

**Coaching on demand** is the single interaction: speech completion, pauses, typed transcript additions and speaker corrections never generate candidate replies. Every button/shortcut request freezes the current Company Brain configuration, client preparation, manager objective, conversation state and finalized speech from both people. In-progress transcript fragments are labeled incomplete and cannot establish facts or commitments. The same request pipeline supports no transcript, rep-only conversation, long buyer explanations and statements without questions. An empty transcript does not imply a cold call: returning technical evaluations use their reviewed history and stage. With sparse optional context the copilot uses a general opening without inventing names or products.

The live card shows **Next move** and **Suggested wording**. Guidance may introduce a purpose, clarify a need, explain supported value, address an objection, explore an authorized tradeoff, revisit an unresolved matter, propose a next step or give the buyer room to respond. The current objective is available beside the request button. Existing suggestions remain readable during generation. Continued speech does not cancel, replace, clear or silently rate them. Clicking again explicitly supersedes the pending request with a fresh snapshot; stale responses and verification cannot overwrite the newest request. Listening continues independently. Timing is measured from the request, rather than from a buyer pause.

Factual grounding and company/client boundaries apply to each explicit request, including its verification and any repair. Source checks can update the AI draft with a visible change record; they never silently overwrite rep edits. Suggested words and repeated requests do not establish that anyone spoke, agreed, sent materials or completed a next step. **Used / Edited / Ignored** remain explicit feedback, and the scrollable coaching history stays with the draft and saved call.

The checklist HUD ticks only explicit buyer transcript evidence or labeled known facts from Call Context. Its next move targets a missing item in the selected stage. A factual or quotation question is answered or explicitly parked against the current requirements; it does not automatically request an email. Stage selection, context and feedback appear in the saved call and Manager Decision Packet.

- Public Google Docs text, Sheets/Slides PDF exports, and supported Drive PDF/text files are supported. Google sign-in-only files are rejected by this link importer.
- Import limits: 8 MB/file, 20 files, two nested folder levels and 400,000 imported characters. Folder listings may be incomplete and always show a warning; skipped files and limits are shown. Import individual file links for anything missing.
- Manufacturer memory supports 500,000 characters. It is saved in browser storage. Source documents remain available for review; only approved knowledge in the call’s pinned configuration supplies company facts to coaching and lookups. Draft, archived and uncertain legacy entries remain inactive. This is searchable saved memory, not permanent model training.
- PDF extraction is AI-assisted. Review quantities, units, prices, certification wording and commercial conditions. Sheets/Slides are read through PDF exports, so nonprinting/hidden content may be absent.
- Company claims require supplied source support. Ordinary small talk and stable general knowledge are allowed. Current external facts require verification. Buyer research does not establish supplier capabilities.
- The sales playbook draws on cited communication research; it does not guarantee trust or conversion. Each coaching card shows stage, possible buyer intent, one sales move, a short English script, optional backup/caution, a collapsible explanation and supporting excerpts. Suspected product claims are held while a source check runs. Unsupported commercial details use a confirmation line, without inventing a response deadline, and are logged as missing company knowledge.
- Your Exact Reply is a scrollable history of buyer turns and suggested responses. The newest response is slightly larger; earlier replies remain readable when the buyer or salesperson speaks. Scrolling up keeps your place until you choose Back to latest. Factual lookups use a small status message without a scripted filler card.
- Used, Edited and Ignored stay in English across saved replies and Chinese coaching mode. Feedback records your choice without creating spoken transcript. Edited opens an editor inside that reply; Save edit preserves it in the call history. Saving the wording as a voice style example is a separate, optional checkbox. Later analysis or automatic speech classification does not overwrite manual feedback or an edited reply.
- Call Context supplies the relationship, background, known buyer facts, unknowns and specific goal for every reply. Openings identify the company and purpose; relevant discovery precedes an email request unless the buyer asks to receive materials. Older introductions and spoken commitments remain available on long calls.
- Call type sets a structure and goal, never an actual outcome. Suggestions and their source snapshots are saved separately from spoken transcript. Showing or editing a suggestion does not establish that you said it or that a buyer agreed.

## Mac calls and company caller name

Computer audio uses the browser's system-audio sharing picker. macOS 14.2+ and a recent supported Chrome are the starting point; browser capabilities and system permissions determine availability. The in-app browser may not offer this feature. Video capture permission is needed for the picker, but screen frames are never sent, displayed or saved by Trade Assist. Only the processed audio track is added to the AI connection. All original capture tracks are stopped when the test/session stops or the page closes. Local playback remains audible to you. Close unrelated audio sources because system audio can include notifications and other apps.

Computer mode runs two separate inputs: playback goes to the buyer connection, and the headset microphone runs a transcription-only connection for your words. Both supply context; neither generates coaching automatically. Only an explicit button/shortcut request generates a suggestion, and further speech does not cancel or replace it. Headphones keep buyer playback out of your microphone; this is source labeling, not biometric voice recognition. Typed messages follow the same on-demand behavior. Other system playback can be labeled Buyer, so keep unrelated audio closed. A real call is still needed to verify your devices’ FaceTime relay.

Buyer questions are extracted from the full current paragraph and consecutive buyer segments for both live and typed coaching. A why-company challenge takes priority over generic incumbent-supplier discovery. Multiple explicit questions allow a slightly longer answer so later answers are not silently truncated; source checks still apply to company facts.

Shared computer audio has a dedicated mono speech path with gentle rumble/hiss filtering, a transparent peak ceiling, and a default 1× gain capped at 2×. It requests 48 kHz capture where supported, without microphone echo cancellation, noise suppression, or automatic gain. The isolated buyer channel uses the fuller transcriber and requests confidence metadata; the separate representative microphone retains its existing transcriber. Cost estimates distinguish both models.

When the buyer channel reports a transcription failure, unreliable words, sustained speech with no text, or a missing final transcript, a prominent **Buyer audio unclear** card suggests asking for a repeat and reminds you to listen carefully. Suspicious fragments cannot establish buyer facts, start translation, or generate an answer to an older question. A clearly unfinished sentence waits for a continuation; complete sentences have no added quality-check delay. Breaths, fillers and deliberate thinking pauses do not produce repeat prompts. A reliable subsequent answer clears the alert; **I heard it clearly** clears it manually without inventing missing transcript words. Confidence and speech detection are imperfect and cannot detect every omitted word or audio that never reaches the capture stream.

For regular monthly AT&T wireless, [AT&T's caller-ID instructions](https://www.att.com/support/article/wireless/KM1002271/) are **account profile → Settings → line → Edit Caller ID → enter display name → Save**. Try your authorized company name; confirm with AT&T if the account doesn't accept it. Allow up to 72 hours. AT&T Prepaid always displays Wireless Caller. Recipient carriers and saved contacts control the final appearance. A manufacturer selection in Trade Assist cannot change the registered caller name per call. No carrier settings are changed by this app. [Apple's Mac calling setup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102405) covers the iPhone relay; this does not require signing up for a separate dialer provider.

## Spending

Default estimated limits are **$3 per call and $50 per calendar month**, editable or disableable in **Connection & audio → Spending & limits**. The meter uses reported token usage and published rates; repeated events are deduplicated. It includes research, document extraction, verified guidance and live/transcription usage recorded by this browser.

At a reached limit the app blocks new AI requests and disconnects the live assistant; your separate phone call can continue. Requests already in progress can still add cost. This is a local estimate guard, not an OpenAI account-wide cap. Other browsers/apps, activity before tracking was added, missing usage reports, taxes and phone charges are excluded. Search and unknown-model estimates can include conservative allowances. OpenAI Platform remains the final billing record.

## Data and connection

Profiles, imported text, voice preferences, buyer briefs, transcripts, call history and usage persist in this browser. They do not sync between `localhost`, `127.0.0.1` or the private hosted entry. Use `127.0.0.1` consistently. Export a backup in settings; imports merge records and usage, restore voice/limits, and recover the draft and buyer brief when no current draft is active.

The API key stays in ignored `.env.local` on the server. Never commit/share it. Relevant company materials and conversation are sent to OpenAI for requested AI work; buyer research uses public web search. Live audio is sent only while listening; raw audio is not saved by this app. Audio tests stay local. Room-microphone modes mix speakers and initially use **Call audio** labels, which can be corrected. Sessions stop after 55 minutes. Response timing varies with speech detection, network, context, knowledge lookup and any factual verification.

The hosted private URL is an entry to the connected local app; no API secret is deployed there. Keep using this Mac’s launcher for live calling.

## Company Brain and learning

Golden Moment review requires an explanation in your own words; the five category tags are optional. The exact explanation stays in that company's Brain and appears in its saved Golden Moment. Similar future moments retrieve the explanation with the historical example for both manual and live suggestions. Matching uses the original stage and moment, with current-item detection for selected-item lessons. All explicit next-move requests use the current model with matching written lessons; no background or automatic local reply bypasses them. Explanations guide response strategy and never authorize unsupported company facts or import old buyer agreements. The shared General Playbook retains placeholder patterns only.

Company Brain keeps the company’s knowledge, manager configuration, canonical client records and historical learning isolated by company. Its three editable sections are **What we know** (facts, products, specifications, supported strengths and terms), **How we sell** (stages, objectives, full playbooks and information to bring back), and **Our boundaries** (permitted actions, required approvals, prohibitions and separate selling preferences). Entries retain relevant units, conditions, dates and sources. Direct manager information records its origin without requiring a document quotation. Legacy records with uncertain factual approval stay pending review rather than becoming silently approved; original documents and historical corrections are preserved.

All manager configuration edits save to a separate draft. **Review changes** shows before/after details and material conflicts; **Apply configuration** creates an identifiable active version. Drafts and applied versions survive reloads. Applied changes affect subsequent calls; an already-bound call keeps its stage, objective, playbook and configuration snapshot. Renamed or retired stages retain stable IDs for historical references. Missing information guides discovery and review without becoming a compulsory questionnaire. Describing a change in your own words proposes editable structured changes with a destination and scope; adding a proposal to the draft does not apply it.

The built-in sales foundation and shared **General Playbook** remain in use alongside active company facts, manager instructions and conversation context. They cannot override current boundaries. A configured stage playbook routes sales moves through the model when interpretation is needed, so an older instant template cannot bypass the manager’s approach. Existing factual and authority checks still apply within explicit requests; there are no automatic documented-answer replies while listening. Learned examples guide strategy and wording but cannot authorize a fact, discount, sample or promise.

Full call reviews, buyer-memory editing and Manager Packet decisions remain in **Call History**, linked from Company Brain’s collapsed **Supporting records**. **Company Brain → Supporting records** retains rated Used/Edited/Ignored review, verified promotion and dataset export; the learning audit and Golden Moments remain in **General Playbook**. The saved call identifies its selected stage and configuration version for review against the correct objective. Full Brains persist in IndexedDB (`trade-assist-brains-v1`); ordinary workspace snapshots omit this larger dataset. Export Brain or the workspace backup to include it. Edited feedback and explanations retrieve relevant stage/moment examples without model training. Company IDs are checked at the request boundary. Risky example promotions require Knowledge verification and remain subordinate to active authorization rules. Historical feedback is migrated with reconstructed context and an explicit unavailable-original-prompt marker.
